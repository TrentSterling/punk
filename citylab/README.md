# HACKERPUNK Citylab 0.1

Streets-first procedural science-fiction city and desktop traversal study for Trent Sterling's HACKERPUNK. Original generated geometry; no Synty or other commercial pack assets. Visual quality is an evaluation target, not a claim of parity with a finished commercial environment pack.

## Run

https://punk.tront.xyz/citylab/ after the repository's Pages deployment. The hosted source pins Three.js 0.180.0 on jsDelivr. The QA artifact contains vendor dependencies, screenshots and test results. The downloadable standalone HTML embeds the renderer for offline use.

## Controls

WASD moves. Hold right mouse to look. Shift moves faster. E/Q or Space/Ctrl flies vertically. F toggles fly/walk. In walk mode E climbs any nearby structural surface without handhold markers or stamina. Space jumps; hold while airborne on augmented equipment to thrust. 1 = human, 2 = bigger jump/jetpack, 3 = 115 m grapple. L toggles low-gravity boots on augmented equipment. Click to capture the mouse, then hold left click to grapple. Escape releases the cursor.

R rerolls the seed; T enters a specific seed; G switches greybox; Alt+D switches dressing; N cycles daylight/overcast/night; O switches ambient occlusion; [ and ] adjust tower height. V cycles five viewpoints. H shows controls. Tab hides the GPU HUD. J exports the city recipe and structural collision. P saves a screenshot.

## Pipeline

Seeded arterial/secondary streets reserve corridors first. Blocks follow road and sidewalk bounds. Concrete masses, towers, courtyards and deck rings are built next. Upper sockets connect adjacent blocks at 16 m. Facades, physical window bays, ribs, machinery, conduits, shopfronts and street furniture dress the structure. Four tower families and an open-frame civic landmark establish height hierarchy. Recipes do not change when visual settings or equipment change. Repeated primitives are instanced; major masses merge by material. UI and world typography are rendered with WebGL textures rather than DOM panels.

## Boundaries

Desktop WebGL 2 prototype. Not a finished game, art pack, or VR locomotion rig. No missions, multiplayer, enterable interiors, automated parkour scoring, chunk streaming, Unity export or Quest/120 Hz verification. Distant skyline is scenery; the playable district has a boundary. AABB collision approximates bevels; fine decoration and decorative moving vehicles are non-colliding. Equipment tiers are preview switches rather than a progression system. Grapple range/strength variants are not itemized yet. SSAO is optional because of its extra rendering cost.

## QA

`qa.cjs` uses Chromium/SwiftShader to capture five perspectives, night and greybox; checks deterministic recipes, road clearance, three additional seeds and basic controls. The workflow is scoped to this directory. The existing landing page is unchanged. Three.js and addons are MIT licensed, with notices retained in vendor source and THREE-LICENSE. All other art is generated by code and canvas typography.
